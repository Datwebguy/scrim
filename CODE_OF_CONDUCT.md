# Code of Conduct

Scrim holds people's money, so we want a project where people feel safe to ask questions, report problems and share ideas.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient. Assume good intent, and ask when something is unclear.
- Give feedback on the work, not the person. Be specific and be helpful.
- Welcome newcomers. Everyone here started somewhere.
- Respect privacy. Never share someone else's personal details, keys or wallet information.

## What we do not accept

- Harassment, insults, threats or discrimination of any kind.
- Unwelcome sexual attention or comments.
- Publishing private information about another person.
- Spam, scams or attempts to trick people into sending money.

## Reporting

If you see or experience behaviour that breaks these rules, open a private report through GitHub's "Report abuse" option on the comment, or open a private advisory using the steps in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Reports are handled privately and taken seriously.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close issues and pull requests, or block people who break these rules.

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), version 2.1.
