# HACKATHON.md

The NEAR HACKATHON runs from 1 November 2026 00:00 UTC to 14 November 2026 23:59 UTC. Judging takes place 16–18 November. Results are announced by 18 November 23:59 UTC. Total prize pool is $250,000 paid in NEAR. Grand prize is $55,000. Thirteen tracks each have first, second, and third places of $10,000, $3,000, and $2,000.

Tracks: Unstoppable DeFi, Investment Thesis, Privacy & Private AI, Autonomous Business, AI Swarms, AI Security & Defense, Agent Economy, Real-World Money, Games with Real Economies, NFTs That Do Something, Memecoins with Mechanics, Social Money, and Open Innovation. A project may fit multiple tracks but submits only once.

No advance registration is required. The only submission is a public repository plus a public demo post on X that states the project was built at NEAR HACKATHON and includes video or screenshots. The submission form or API (POST https://nearhackathon.com/api/applications) requires project name, lead name, email, team size, tracks, NEAR account for payout, repository URL, demo URL, a 20–2000 character description, and consent. The repository must be public by the deadline.

Official site: https://nearhackathon.com
