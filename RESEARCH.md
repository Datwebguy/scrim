# RESEARCH.md

NEAR Business launched 20 October 2026 as a confidential multisig treasury for crypto-native teams. It keeps transaction details private while remaining auditable. Funds sit in Intents; approval rules live in the Treasury Contract. Site: https://business.near.com. Official article: https://x.com/NEARProtocol/article/2112544505891070286.

Confidential Intents is generally available. near.com defaults to confidential holdings, trades, payments, and yield. Confidential TVL overtook public TVL in September. Limit orders and confidential perps shipped. Robinhood Chain support was added.

A $3.8 million Intents contract issue related to a deposit notification feature was identified, funds returned, the feature removed, and additional invariant checks added. Postmortem published mid-October 2026.

Post-quantum signing (ML-DSA) is live. Dynamic resharding shipped. MPC network expanded and put on protocol funding.

Hackathon posts from 19–21 October 2026 detail the tracks, timeline, and submission rules. Official site and HACKATHON.md contain the current requirements: https://nearhackathon.com and https://nearhackathon.com/HACKATHON.md.
